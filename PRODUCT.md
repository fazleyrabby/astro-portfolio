# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Fazley Rabbi's personal portfolio presents his backend engineering work, career, technical capabilities, and writing. Visitors can explore projects, read articles, view his resume, and contact him.

## Users

Primary audience remains an open decision. The redesign should make engineering work easy to assess without requiring a long scroll.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve real profile, project, career, and article content and working links.
- Preserve English and Bengali routes.
- Keep one navigation, a simple career history, four projects in a simple grid, and Technical Capabilities in a separate card with space around it.
- Show only the latest four published articles where available.
- Omit the experiments section and the previously removed narrative introductions.
- Preserve the request demonstration, contact options, and resume access.
- Develop the redesign on the separate `codex/editorial-portfolio` branch.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing profile data, portrait, project records and images, experience timeline, technical skills, published articles, and request tracer in apps/web.

## Product Principles

- Make the homepage compact and easy to scan.
- Let actual work explain the engineer's experience.
- Preserve factual content rather than inventing achievements.

## Brand Commitments

The user rejected the editorial redesign. Use a modern appearance with subtle animation and retain both light and dark mode.
