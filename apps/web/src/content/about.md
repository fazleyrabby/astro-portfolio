## Building reliable software, one system at a time.

I'm a Backend Engineer with 5+ years of experience building production-grade backend systems using Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Docker, and modern web technologies.

My work focuses on designing scalable APIs, payment infrastructure, fraud detection, automation, analytics pipelines, and backend architecture for products serving thousands of daily users. I enjoy solving engineering problems where software has a direct business impact and building systems that remain reliable long after deployment.

## Engineering Philosophy

I'm interested in how software works beneath the surface. Beyond frameworks, I enjoy studying system design, distributed systems, infrastructure, and the architectural decisions that make applications scalable, maintainable, and dependable.

I believe great software should be simple to understand, easy to maintain, and built with long-term reliability in mind.
